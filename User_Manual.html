3. Detailed Workflows Manual by Role

Step-by-step procedures for operating modules in PROP-MANAGER.

A. Property Listing Creation & Approval Workflow

1
Property Draft Creator (Branch Admin)

Branch Admin logs in with pune_admin and navigates to Add Property Draft (/properties/new).

  • Fills in Project Name, Property Type, Builder, City, Suburb, and Address.
  • Adds BHK configurations (Type, Carpet Area, Total Price, Est. EMI).
  • Adds technical specifications, checks amenities, and attaches photos/brochure files.
  • Saves property record as a Draft status.
2
Draft Submission for Review (Branch Admin)

On the Property Catalog (/properties), the Branch Admin locates the saved draft and clicks Submit for Approval.

  • The property status transitions from Draft to Pending Approval.
  • The property is routed to the Super Admin Approvals Queue.
3
Audit & Review (Super Admin)

Super Admin logs in with superadmin and accesses Approvals Queue (/approvals).

  • Audits project configurations, specs, RERA ID, and file attachments.
  • Clicks Approve: Property status becomes Approved and is instantly published to Sales Executives & External Brokers across matching branches.
  • Clicks Reject: Property is flagged Rejected and returned to the Branch Admin for revisions.

B. BeyondWalls External Broker Portal & Referral Workflow

1
Broker Login & Portal Dashboard

External Broker logs in with broker_maharashtra and lands on the customized BeyondWalls Broker Portal.

  • Views categorized sections: Residential Launches, Commercial Complexes, and Geographic Collections (Pune / Mumbai).
  • Only properties belonging to allowed branches (e.g. Pune Kondhwa Branch) are visible.
2
Property Share & PDF Brochure Download

Broker clicks View Details on any property (e.g. Sai Sanskruti Phase 2).

  • Clicks WhatsApp Share or Email Share: Enters recipient contact info. System generates direct links and logs activity in audit stream.
  • Clicks Download Brochure: Downloads official PDF marketing material. System records brochure download event.
3
Client Referral Lead Submission

Broker clicks Refer Client Lead on property detail page or uses Lead referral submission button.

  • Enters buyer client name, phone number, email, and specific requirement notes.
  • Submits inquiry: Lead is registered in system database and automatically routed to Branch Sales Executives.
  • Broker tracks status in My Referrals (/leads).

C. Sales Executive Lead Processing & Status Conversion Workflow

1
Lead Referral Inbox Inspection

Sales Executive logs in with pune_executive and accesses Lead Referral Tracker (/leads).

  • Executive views leads assigned to their branch and account.
  • Inspects referral broker name, buyer phone/email, property code, and requirement notes.
2
Status Conversion Transitions

Executive updates the status dropdown menu as buyer negotiations progress:

  • New: Initial inquiry received from broker referral.
  • In Progress: Site visit scheduled or quote sent.
  • Converted: Deal finalized, token amount received!
  • Closed/Lost: Buyer opted out or selected another project.

D. Staff Directory & External Broker Management Workflow

1
Staff User Registration (/users)

Super Admin or Branch Admin registers internal employees (Assistant Admins, Branch Admins, Sales Executives) and assigns branch affiliations.

2
External Broker Provisioning (/brokers)

Admins create third-party broker profiles, check allowed regional branches (e.g. Pune / Mumbai), toggle active/deactivated status, and view live broker audit logs.
