360° Virtual Tour Creation & Publishing Guide

Step-by-step instructions on capturing 360° spherical property photos, hosting interactive walkthrough tours, and embedding them into the Real Estate Management System.

Jump to Section: 1. Capture & Creation 2. Hosting Platforms 3. Add to Property Form 4. Live Embed Examples

Why Add 360° Virtual Tours?

Virtual 360° tours allow buyers and external channel partner brokers to virtually walk through every room of a property (Living Room, Kitchen, Bedrooms, Balcony) from anywhere in the world. Listings with 360° tours receive 3x higher client engagement and significantly accelerate buyer decision-making before physical site visits.

1 Phase 1: How to Capture & Create 360° Content

Choose one of the three standard capture methods based on your available equipment and property status:

Method A: Smartphone Apps
Free & Easy

No special hardware needed! Use your existing iPhone or Android smartphone.

  1. Download Polycam, 360 Panorama, or Google Street View App.
  2. Stand in the middle of the room.
  3. Rotate 360° following the on-screen target dots until the entire room is captured.
  4. Save/Export as a 360° Equirectangular Panoramic JPG.
Method B: Dedicated 360 Camera
Professional Quality

Ideal for sales teams and real estate photographers.

  1. Use a 360° camera such as Insta360 ONE X2 / X3, Ricoh Theta Z1, or GoPro MAX.
  2. Mount camera on a slim tripod in room center.
  3. Trigger shot remotely via smartphone Wi-Fi app.
  4. Export instant 5.7K crystal-clear 360° images.
Method C: 3D Renderings
Under-Construction / Unbuilt

Perfect for upcoming launches and unbuilt sample flats.

  1. Ask your 3D Architectural / VR Designer.
  2. Request 3D VR renders exported as Spherical 360° Panorama PNGs (V-Ray 360 Camera render).
  3. Renders can be uploaded directly to tour hosts just like photos!

2 Phase 2: Uploading & Getting the Share / Embed Link

Once you have your 360° panoramic images, upload them to an interactive tour hosting platform:

Option 1: Kuula.co (Recommended - Free & Fast)
Kuula Tour Host
  1. Go to Kuula.co and create a free account.
  2. Click + Post / Upload and drag & drop your 360° room images.
  3. Add hotspots connecting rooms (e.g. click doorway to navigate from *Living Room ➔ Kitchen*).
  4. Click Share button at top right ➔ Click Get Embed Code / Link.
  5. Copy the share URL e.g.: https://kuula.co/share/collection/7yX9P?logo=1&info=1
Option 2: Matterport 3D
3D Spatial Scan

If using Matterport 3D camera scanner, copy the share link from your Matterport Cloud portal:

https://my.matterport.com/show/?m=sampleID
Option 3: YouTube 360° Video
360 Video Embed

Upload a 360 video walkthrough to YouTube and copy the standard embed URL:

https://www.youtube.com/embed/sampleVideoID

3 Phase 3: Adding the Link into Property Form

Step-by-Step Publishing Instructions
  1. Log in as Super Admin or Branch Admin.
  2. Go to Property Catalog (`/properties`) in the sidebar menu.
  3. Click + Add Property (or click Edit on an existing property).
  4. Scroll down to the 🥽 360° VIRTUAL TOUR EMBED LINK section.
  5. Paste your copied Kuula or Matterport URL into the input field:
    https://kuula.co/share/collection/7yX9P?logo=1&info=1
  6. Click Submit Property / Save Changes.
